## `🌊 flagship_mission`

### Underwater detection that survives the move from pool to sea

In collaboration with **LABUST / University of Zagreb FER**, I am extending an
underwater PPE and debris detector beyond its original pool-domain evaluation.

```text
MISSION CONTROL
│
├── PUBLIC BASE
│   ├── TrashCan + SeaClear
│   └── 12,953 train / 2,869 validation images
│
├── DATA AUDIT
│   ├── 2,646 delivered exports
│   ├── 1,206 distinct source frames
│   └── augmentation + sequence leakage identified
│
├── CORRECTED BENCHMARK
│   ├── deduplicated by source frame
│   ├── split by complete source video
│   └── separate cross-platform camera test
│
├── REPRODUCIBILITY
│   ├── deterministic source conversion
│   ├── provenance and environment ledgers
│   └── per-class / per-domain evaluation
│
└── NEXT TRANSMISSION
    ├── full 100-epoch public baseline
    └── leakage-free annotation-efficiency curve
```
